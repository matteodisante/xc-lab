\section{Group or place? Solo flights where groups fly}\label{sec:zones}

Do groups fly where, and when, any pilot would progress straighter? If so, solo flights compared on
the same ground and at the same hours would match group time.

\paragraph{Reweighting.} Each piece gets a stratum from its middle, in four stratifications from coarse
to fine: zone, zone$\times$month, zone$\times$hour, site-day$\times$hour. The solo flights are then given
the mix of the group over the strata (direct standardisation):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reweight}
  \MSD_{\Sl\mid\G}(\tau)=\frac{\sum_s n_{\G,s}\,\MSD_{\Sl,s}(\tau)}{\sum_s n_{\G,s}},
\end{equation}
with $n_{\G,s}$ the units of $\G$ in stratum $s$ (a flight counts once per stratum it crosses) and
$\MSD_{\Sl,s}$ the ensemble MSD of the solo units of $s$. In the statistic, $\Sl$ then flies where
and when $\G$ flies. $\G$ is taken on the strata it shares with $\Sl$, so the two curves have the same
support; the same is done for $\A$. ``As is'' is the comparison on the same strata without the
reweighting.

\paragraph{Results.} The gap opens above $\tau\approx150$~s (Figure~\ref{fig:zones-diff}): in every
stratification $\Delta\Hloc$ between $\G$ and $\Sl$ lies between $\ZoneShortMin$ and
$\ZoneShortMax$ from 20 to 140~s, and pooled it reaches \ZoneDHlocSNone{} at 1000~s, above one
climb--glide cycle. Reweighting removes little of it. To the zones of $\G$, it lowers
$\Delta\Hloc(1000~\mathrm{s})$ by \ZoneRedLocZone\% (\ZoneDHlocSupportZone{} $\to$ \ZoneDHlocSZone) and
the fitted $\Delta H$ by \ZoneRedHZone\%; with zone$\times$month, by \ZoneRedLocZoneMonth\% and
\ZoneRedHZoneMonth\%; with zone$\times$hour, by \ZoneRedLocZoneHour\% and \ZoneRedHZoneHour\%. Solo
pilots do fly where groups fly: \ZoneKeptZone\% of $\G$ has a solo counterpart in its cell. At the
same site-day and hour (\ZoneKeptSdh\% of $\G$ kept), $\Delta\Hloc(1000~\mathrm{s})$ is \ZoneDHlocSSdh{}
\ZoneDHlocSSdhBand, \ZoneRedLocSdh\% below its value as is, and the fitted $\Delta H$ is \ZoneDHSSdh{}
\ZoneDHSSdhBand. Reweighting raises the fitted $H$ of $\Sl$ by a few thousandths (\ZoneHNoneS{} $\to$
\ZoneHSdhS) and never brings it to that of $\G$ (\ZoneHSdhG).

Against the same flights alone, $\Delta\Hloc(1000~\mathrm{s})$ is \ZoneDHlocANone{} pooled and
\ZoneDHlocAZone, \ZoneDHlocAZoneHour{} and \ZoneDHlocASdh{} in zone, zone$\times$hour and
site-day$\times$hour strata. At the same site-day and hour these values differ from the contrasts of
Section~\ref{sec:other} on the same strata (\ZoneDHlocASdh{} here against \DHlocThousandGroupSdh{}
there) only through the weights, $n_{\G,s}$ here and the half harmonic mean there: the difference
varies between strata, and the group effect at the same site-day and hour lies between the two.

\paragraph{Limits.} Cells are 25~km, placed by the middle of a piece; the terrain inside a cell is
not controlled, and a group may choose better air within it, which is then part of what we call the
group effect. Paragliders only.

\paragraph{What we keep.} The zones, months and hours where groups fly explain a fifth to a quarter of
the gap with solo flights; the rest stays at the same site, day and hour. Inside a zone, the route
itself remains to be tested.
