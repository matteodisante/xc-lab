\section{Group or place? Solo flights where groups fly}\label{sec:zones}

Do groups fly where, and when, any pilot would progress straighter? If so, solo flights compared on
the same ground and at the same hours would match group time.

\paragraph{Reweighting.} Each piece of track gets a stratum from its middle, from coarse to fine:
zone, zone$\times$month, zone$\times$hour, site-day$\times$hour. The solo flights are then given the
mix of the group over the strata (direct standardisation):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reweight}
  \MSD_{\Sl\mid\G}(\tau)=\frac{\sum_s n_{\G,s}\,\MSD_{\Sl,s}(\tau)}{\sum_s n_{\G,s}},
\end{equation}
with $n_{\G,s}$ the units of $\G$ in stratum $s$ (a flight counts once per stratum it crosses) and
$\MSD_{\Sl,s}$ the ensemble MSD of the solo units of $s$. In the statistic, $\Sl$ then flies where
and when $\G$ flies. $\G$ is taken on the same strata, so the two curves share their support; the
same is done for $\A$. ``As is'' is the comparison on the same strata without the reweighting.

\paragraph{Results.} The gap opens above $\tau\approx150$~s (Figure~\ref{fig:zones-diff}):
$\Delta\Hloc$ between $\G$ and $\Sl$ is $\ZoneShortMin$ to $\ZoneShortMax$ from 20 to 140~s and
\ZoneDHlocSNone{} at 1000~s, above one climb--glide cycle. The group therefore acts on how the cycles
are chained, while a single glide or climb is unchanged. Reweighting to the zones of $\G$ lowers
$\Delta\Hloc(1000~\mathrm{s})$ by \ZoneRedLocZone\% (\ZoneDHlocSupportZone{} $\to$ \ZoneDHlocSZone) and
the fitted $\Delta H$ by \ZoneRedHZone\%; adding the month, by \ZoneRedLocZoneMonth\% and
\ZoneRedHZoneMonth\%; adding the hour, by \ZoneRedLocZoneHour\% and \ZoneRedHZoneHour\%. Solo pilots
do fly where groups fly: \ZoneKeptZone\% of $\G$ has a solo counterpart in its cell. At the same
site-day and hour (\ZoneKeptSdh\% of $\G$ kept), $\Delta\Hloc(1000~\mathrm{s})=\ZoneDHlocSSdh$
\ZoneDHlocSSdhBand, \ZoneRedLocSdh\% below its value as is, and the fitted $\Delta H=\ZoneDHSSdh$
\ZoneDHSSdhBand: reweighting raises the fitted $H$ of $\Sl$ by a few thousandths (\ZoneHNoneS{} $\to$
\ZoneHSdhS) and never brings it to that of $\G$ (\ZoneHSdhG). Against the same flights alone,
$\Delta\Hloc(1000~\mathrm{s})$ is \ZoneDHlocANone{} pooled and \ZoneDHlocAZone, \ZoneDHlocAZoneHour{}
and \ZoneDHlocASdh{} in zone, zone$\times$hour and site-day$\times$hour strata. Below 150~s the sign
of $\G-\A$ depends on the hour, which is why the two estimates of Section~\ref{sec:other} are read
together.

\paragraph{Limits.} Cells are 25~km, placed by the middle of a piece; the terrain inside a cell is
not controlled, and a group may choose better air within it, which is then part of what we call the
group effect. Paragliders only.

\paragraph{What we keep.} The place and the hour where groups fly explain at most a fifth of the
gap; the rest stays at the same site, day and hour.
