Group time progresses straighter over long lags than solo flights,
$\Delta\Hloc(1000~\mathrm{s})=\DHlocThousandRaw$; it also spends more time climbing, ends more
searches in a climb and glides faster (Table~\ref{tab:raw}; the curves over every lag are in
Appendix~\ref{app:curves}). The same flights alone sit between the two at long lags and have the
highest climb rate. Part of the raw difference may therefore belong to who flies in groups, and to
where and when they fly.

\paragraph{What we keep.} A difference to explain, largest at long lags. The next three sections
ask how much of it is the group.
