\subsection{The moment: the same flight before, during and after}\label{sec:prepost}

\textbf{Question.} Pilots may fly together in the moments and places where the progress is straight anyway. If so, the
same flight would already be straight just before the group forms and still just after it breaks. \textbf{Method.}
Every stretch of group time lasting at least \PPWindowMin{} min is an \emph{event} with up to four windows of
\PPWindowMin{} min of the flight clock: \emph{before} (in the solo time of the same flight), \emph{start} and \emph{end}
of the group, \emph{after}. Before and after need a solo stretch of that length next to the group. For two windows the
ensemble MSD is taken on the events that have both. \textbf{Controls:} two consecutive windows with no change of state,
in the solo time of the group flights (A) and in solo flights (S): the drift of a flight between consecutive windows.

\begin{reading}
\textbf{Reading.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Straightness follows the group.} $\Delta H_\mathrm{loc}(1000)$ is \PPEntryDHloc{} \PPEntryDHlocBand{}
        entering and \PPExitDHloc{} \PPExitDHlocBand{} leaving, against \PPCtrlADHloc{} and \PPCtrlSDHloc{} in the controls.
  \item \textbf{Before and after are alike:} after minus before is \PPAfterBeforeDHloc{} \PPAfterBeforeDHlocBand. On the
        \PPProfileEvents{} events with all four windows $H_\mathrm{loc}(1000)$ is \PPProfileHlocPre{} before,
        \PPProfileHlocStart{} and \PPProfileHlocEnd{} in the group, \PPProfileHlocPost{} after; the step matches the
        within-flight estimate of Section~\ref{sec:other} (\DHlocThousandFlight).
  \item \textbf{Nothing within one glide or climb:} at 100~s \PPEntryDHlocHundred{} entering and \PPExitDHlocHundred{}
        leaving, inside the drift of the controls (\PPCtrlADHlocHundred{}, \PPCtrlSDHlocHundred{}). The fitted $H$
        (\PPEntryDH, \PPExitDH{} against \PPCtrlADH, \PPCtrlSDH) does not separate from the drift, because its slope is
        dominated by the lags below 150~s.
  \item \textbf{Glides are faster and flatter in the group, both ways:} glide speed \PPEntrySpeed{} km/h entering and
        \PPExitSpeed{} leaving, height lost per km \PPEntrySink{} and \PPExitSink{} m/km, against \PPCtrlSSpeed{} and
        \PPCtrlASpeed{} km/h, \PPCtrlASink{} and \PPCtrlSSink{} m/km in the controls. Time in climb, successful searches
        and climb rate move by the size of the drift: no robust change.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Limits.} The windows fix the pilot, the glider, the day and the slow drift of the flight, not the moment: what
brings pilots together (a convergence line, a ridge, a common route) may last as long as the group and straighten the
progress by itself, and two pilots must move alike to stay within 500~m for \PPWindowMin{} min. Events are long groups
with solo flight beside them (\PPEntryEvents{} entries, \PPExitEvents{} exits); phases on 1~Hz flights only. Windows of
\PPShortWindowMin{} min: Appendix~\ref{app:prepost}.
\end{reading}

\textbf{What we keep.} On the same flight the straighter progress starts with the group and stops with it.
