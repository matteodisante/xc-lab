\section{What can and cannot be concluded}\label{sec:conclusions}

The raw difference between group time and solo flights is real, and most of it survives the three
tests. Fixing the pilot, the day, the site and the hour leaves a straighter progress over a quarter
of an hour, more climbing and faster, flatter glides (Section~\ref{sec:other}); the zones and hours
where groups fly explain at most a fifth of the gap (Section~\ref{sec:zones}); on the same flight the
effect starts and stops with the group (Section~\ref{sec:prepost}). Within one glide or climb nothing
changes: the group acts on how the climbs and glides follow each other, at the scale of several
cycles. Two pilots carry most of it (Section~\ref{sec:inside}).

\medskip
\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.49\textwidth}
\textbf{Supported}
\begin{itemize}
  \item the progress over a quarter of an hour is straighter in a group, with the pilot, the day, the
        site and the hour fixed;
  \item no change within one glide or climb (100~s);
  \item on the same flight the straighter progress starts and stops with the group, and before and
        after are alike;
  \item more time climbing, more searches that end in a climb, a higher climb rate, faster and
        flatter glides once stratified (the pooled climb rate pointed the other way);
  \item selection of pilots explains little of the motion;
  \item the pilot ahead flies a little faster but finds no more thermals than the follower.
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.49\textwidth}
\textbf{Not supported}
\begin{itemize}
  \item that the group \emph{causes} the effect: the tests fix pilot, day, site, hour, zone and the
        drift of the flight, but not what brings pilots together (a line, a ridge, a route) nor the
        coordination needed to stay in contact;
  \item that larger groups straighten the progress, up to six pilots;
  \item that a contact means interaction: it means shared air within sight;
  \item the glide statistics are partly fixed by definition, since headings must be aligned in
        glide for a contact;
  \item the leader is assigned from $\ell_{\rm med}$, which is uncertain in thermals (note,
        Appendix~B).
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}

\paragraph{Open.} The phases exist on 1~Hz flights only: the same analyses on the tracks of
xc-dynamics, with phases on all loggers, and on hang gliders with enough group flights
(Appendix~\ref{app:curves}), remain to be done.
