\section{Uncertainty bands}\label{app:bands}

A bootstrap over flights treats them as independent, and they are not. A pilot flies many flights.
Flights of one site and day share the air, and so do the members of one episode. A block is a set
of flights tied by a shared episode or by a shared site-day, with chained ties; two flights of the
same day share a site-day when their takeoffs are within \LtSiteKm~km. A flight tied to no other is
a block of its own.

To first order an estimate $\hat\theta$ is a sum of terms $\psi_k$ over the units $k$ of the data
(flights, pieces or events). Two terms are correlated when their units share a pilot $p$ or
a block $b$, so
\begin{multline}
V=\sum_{k,l}\psi_k\psi_l\,\mathbf{1}[p_k=p_l\ \text{or}\ b_k=b_l]\\
=V_P+V_B-V_C ,
\end{multline}
where $V_P$ sums the pairs of units with the same pilot, $V_B$ those with the same block, and $V_C$
those with the same pilot and block, which $V_P$ and $V_B$ both count~\cite{Cameron2011}. Each term
is the variance of a one-way cluster bootstrap. We resample with replacement the pilots, the blocks
and the cells (a cell is a pilot in a block), with \NBoot{} draws of each. In a draw every unit
weighs the number of times its pilot (block, cell) is drawn, and every statistic is recomputed as on
the data. All the quantities of one design use the same draws, so a difference of two quantities has
the band of its own draws. With $V_P$, $V_B$ and $V_C$ the variances of the estimate over the three
sets of draws,
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
V&=\max\bigl(V_P+V_B-V_C,\;V_P,\;V_B\bigr),\\
\text{band}&=\hat\theta\pm z_{0.95}\sqrt{V},
\end{split}
\end{equation}
a 90\% band, with $z_{0.95}$ the 0.95 quantile of the standard normal distribution. The maximum
keeps $V$ above each one-way variance when noise makes the sum smaller.

We checked the coverage on synthetic data. In the three designs of the letter we kept the real
flights, pilots, blocks, pieces and events, and replaced the value of every unit by
$y=a_\text{pilot}+b_\text{block}+c_\text{flight}+e_\text{unit}$, with independent normal effects and
no group effect, in several mixes of their variances, \CovReplicates{} replicates each and \CovDraws{}
draws per bootstrap. A 90\% band must hold 0 in 90\% of the replicates. Ours holds it in
\CovNewMin--\CovNewMax\%.
