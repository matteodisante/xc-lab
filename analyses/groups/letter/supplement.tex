\section{The three designs}
\pending{Raw; same route (route match, calipers, the counts, selection $A - S|A$); same flight
(windows, $D_\mathrm{in}$, $D_\mathrm{out}$, $g$, $d_w$, window length, controls; $g$ at 1840~s
with windows of 2400 and 3000~s, \PreWinCHQ{} and \PreWinDHQ); the opposite
signs of glide speed and height lost per km between the same-route and same-flight designs.}

\emph{Matching.} A piece of group flight matches a piece of a solo flight when a share \MatCover{}
of its points lies within \MatRho~m of the solo track, flown in the same direction, within
\MatMonth{} months of the date and \MatHour{} hours of the time of day, on a wing of the same class.

\emph{Longer lags.} At 1840~s, on the longer pieces, the same flights while alone differ from their
matched solo pieces by \MatDeltaHQA{} \MatDeltaHQABand{} (\MatDeltaHKA{} \MatDeltaHKABand{} at
1000~s), and the effect of the group at fixed route and pilot is \MatDeltaHQD{} \MatDeltaHQDBand.

\emph{Route alone.} In a wider match, on the route alone and over all flights, the gap of the
matched pieces against the solo flights of their site, day and hour falls from \RouteBaseDHloc{} to
\RouteDHloc{} \RouteDHlocBand{} at 1000~s, by \RouteExplained\%, for every matching radius and cover
we tried.

\emph{Controls of the same flight.} Two consecutive windows drawn at random in solo time show no
change: \PreCAHK{} \PreCAHKBand{} in the same flights alone and \PreCSHK{} \PreCSHKBand{} in solo
flights. The mock events are \PreMockEvents{} sets of four windows in \PreMockFlights{} flights
never in a group, for \PreMockRealEvents{} of the events; on them $g$ is \PreMockGHK{}
\PreMockGHKBand. With mock events from the same site it is \PreMockSiteGHK{} \PreMockSiteGHKBand,
and $g$ minus mock is \PreNetSiteGHK{} \PreNetSiteGHKBand.

\emph{Cross-country speed.} The straight-line distance covered in 30 min from every origin, over
30 min, on the pieces of at least 2250~s. Raw: G \RawStatXcG{} \RawStatXcGBand, A \RawStatXcA{}
\RawStatXcABand, S \RawStatXcS{} \RawStatXcSBand~km/h; G $-$ S \RawGapXc{} \RawGapXcBand, A $-$ S
\RawGapXcAS{} \RawGapXcASBand, G $-$ A \RawGapXcGA{} \RawGapXcGABand~km/h. Same route, on
\MatXcPiecesG{} pieces of G and \MatXcPiecesA{} of A: G $-$ S$|$G \MatDeltaXcG{} \MatDeltaXcGBand,
A $-$ S$|$A \MatDeltaXcA{} \MatDeltaXcABand, difference \MatDeltaXcD{} \MatDeltaXcDBand~km/h. Same
flight: $g$ \PreGXc{} \PreGXcBand, $d_w$ \PreDwXc{} \PreDwXcBand, mock events \PreMockGXc{}
\PreMockGXcBand, $g$ minus mock \PreNetGXc{} \PreNetGXcBand~km/h.

\emph{Phases.} Raw, a search in a group ends in a climb \RawStatFoundG\% of the time against
\RawStatFoundS\% alone. On the same route, net of selection, the time in climb grows by
\MatDeltaClimbD{} \MatDeltaClimbDBand{} percentage points, while glide speed and height lost per km
do not change (\MatDeltaGlideD{} \MatDeltaGlideDBand~km/h and \MatDeltaSinkD{}
\MatDeltaSinkDBand~m per km). Within the flight, joining a group raises the time in climb by
\PreGClimb{} points and the share of searches that end in a climb by \PreGFound{} points.

\section{Tail of transitions}
\pending{Hill estimator~\cite{Hill1975}, the threshold~\cite{Clauset2009}, bands; descriptors of
the long transitions.}

The transitions between climbs last longer in a group (Fig.~\ref{fig:tail}). Above \LtTailXmin~s
the Hill index of their durations~\cite{Hill1975} is \LtTailMuG{} \LtTailMuGBand{} for G against
\LtTailMuS{} \LtTailMuSBand{} for S, and the two bands stay apart for every threshold between
\LtTailApartFrom{} and \LtTailApartTo~s; climbs and searches show no such difference. Since the
index changes with the threshold, we use it only to compare the weight of the tails. The longest
transitions are flights along lines of rising air: those above \LtLongMin~s cover about
\LtLongPathG~km in a group with a median vertical speed of \LtLongVzG~m/s, rising \LtLongRisingG\%
of the time. In a group they cost less height, \LtLongSinkG{} against \LtLongSinkS~m per km of net
displacement, a gap of \LtLongSinkGapG{} \LtLongSinkGapGBand{} (Fig.~\ref{fig:tail}c). The same
flights alone lie in between, \LtLongSinkGapA{} \LtLongSinkGapABand{} from solo flights, so about
half of the gap comes with the pilots and the days; the gain in vertical speed, \LtLongVzGapG{}
\LtLongVzGapGBand~m/s, appears only in the group (alone, \LtLongVzGapA{} \LtLongVzGapABand). On the
same route the tails of the transitions of G and of its matched solo pieces cannot be told apart,
though only \LtTailRouteRunsG{} group transitions exceed \LtTailXmin~s there.

\section{Terrain}
\pending{Hammond classes~\cite{Hammond1964} in the computer version of Ref.~\cite{Dikau1991} on the
IGN BD ALTI at 25~m resampled to 200~m; the class of a piece; flights that change class.}

Raw, the gain in $\Hloc(1000~\mathrm{s})$ is \LtTerRawLow{} \LtTerRawLowBand{} in plains and hills,
\LtTerRawLowMtn{} \LtTerRawLowMtnBand{} in low mountains and \LtTerRawHigh{} \LtTerRawHighBand{} in
high mountains. Within the same flight it is \LtTerFlightMtn{} \LtTerFlightMtnBand{} in mountains
and \LtTerFlightLow{} \LtTerFlightLowBand{} in plains and hills, where only
\LtTerFlightLowEvents{} events of \LtTerFlightLowPilots{} pilots are available. In plains and hills
routes rarely repeat from one day to another: only \LtTerRouteLowPieces{} pieces of group flight
find a solo flight on the same route.
