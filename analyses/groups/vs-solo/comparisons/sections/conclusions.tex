\begin{itemize}
  \item Many flights declared open fold back on themselves, and the longest lags rest on those. An opening above \MinOpening{} drops the ones that come back near their takeoff and keeps nearly the same share of lowland and mountain flights.
  \item \textbf{Raw.} On the open flights group time progresses straighter than solo flight: $\Hk$ \RawHKOneG{} in $\G$, \RawHKOneS{} in $\Sl$, \RawHKOneA{} in $\A$, and it has more time in climb (\RawStatClimbG\% against \RawStatClimbS\%) and a faster glide
  (\RawStatGlideG{} against \RawStatGlideS~km/h). The bands of $\G$ and $\Sl$ do not overlap for $\Hk$, nor for \RawSepCount{} of the 7 statistics.
  \item \textbf{Same route, season, hour and wing.} The gap of $\Hk$ is \MatDeltaHKG{} \MatDeltaHKGBand, of $\Hq$ \MatDeltaHQG{} \MatDeltaHQGBand. The bands include 0 for \MatZeroG{} of the \MatNQuant{} quantities, and those of the selection term
  $\Delta(\A-\SAA)$ for \MatZeroA{} of them. The data do not separate group time from its controls on the same route.
  \item \textbf{Decomposition.} The route carries the largest part of $\Delta(\emptyset)$ for time in climb, glide speed and height lost per km of glide; season and hour carry almost none, and the wing a small part. $\Delta(\emptyset)$ differs from the raw gap
  because of the pieces, the controls without a route and the unit. At $\Hk$ it is near 0 and no share is given.
  \item \textbf{Before and after.} The drift-free effect $g$ of $\Hk$ is \PreGHK{} \PreGHKBand, inside the band of the same-route gap, \PreVsGapHK{} above its point value. Time in climb, searches ending in a climb and climbs per hour
  agree in sign with the same-route gap. Time in search, climb rate, glide speed and height lost per km of glide have the opposite sign; we give no reason for it.
\end{itemize}

\noindent Where a band includes 0 (Table~\ref{tab:summary}, $^\circ$) the comparison does not tell the sign.
