\section{Aim}\label{sec:aim}

Does a paraglider move differently in a group than alone? This report compares group time with solo
flight on open flights, one step at a time, and reads each result together with the number of flights
behind it, given in every table and figure.

Section~\ref{sec:flights} defines the flights, the populations and the measures. Section~\ref{sec:declared}
compares the populations as they are on the flights the pilots declare open, and finds that their mean
squared displacement bends down at long lags. Section~\ref{sec:opening} shows why: many declared open
flights fold back on themselves, and the longest lags rest on those. It sets a threshold on how open a
flight is, and Section~\ref{sec:open} repeats the comparison on the flights that pass it.
