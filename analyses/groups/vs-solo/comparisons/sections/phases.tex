\subsection{Climbs and glides}\label{sec:phases}

Does a group climb and glide differently from the same route flown alone?

Table~\ref{tab:phases} compares the time in each phase and the statistics of climbs and glides of the
pieces that have phases (loggers of 1~s) with the same statistics on their controls. No difference is
beyond its band. Group time has a little less time in glide and more in climb, and more climbs per hour; the
climb rate, the glide speed, the glide ratio and the height lost per kilometre of glide are the same.
