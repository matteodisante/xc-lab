\subsection{Climbs and glides}\label{sec:phases}

Does a group climb and glide differently from the same route flown alone?

Table~\ref{tab:phases} compares the time in each phase and the statistics of climbs and glides of the
pieces that have phases (loggers of 1~s) with the same statistics on their controls. Group time has
more time in climb and less in glide, and its pilots start more climbs per hour; the climb rate, the
glide speed, the glide ratio and the height lost per kilometre of glide differ by less than their
bands. In a group the pilot spends more time climbing, and the climb itself is not faster.

\paragraph{What we keep.} On the same route a group spends more time climbing and less gliding;
the speed, the rate and the glide ratio do not differ.
