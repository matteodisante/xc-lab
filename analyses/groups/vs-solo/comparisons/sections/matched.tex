\section{Point 2: the same route, season, hour and wing}\label{sec:matched}

Does group time still differ from solo flight when the solo side flies the same route, in the same season and hour, with the same wing?

\paragraph{Controls.} For each piece of $\G$ (a stretch in a group, at least \RawShortestOne~s) the controls are solo segments of the pool, the open flights
that never had a contact of any length, on another date than the piece. A segment matches when at least \MatCover{} of the points of the piece, one every 30~s, have the pool track within
\MatRho~m, the pool flight flew them in the same direction, and the segment lasts at least as long as the dataset needs (Appendix~\ref{app:route}). The match table keeps every match with a cover of at least 0.7, the lowest of the robustness lines: \MatMatches{} matches,
from \MatPool{} pool flights; \MatWithMatchG{} pieces of $\G$ and \MatWithMatchA{} pieces of $\A$ have at least one.
A caliper then keeps the matches of the same season (month), the same hour and the same wing.

$\pm\MatMonth$, hour $\pm\MatHour$, the same wing class (\MatMinBeg{} pieces for beginners, \MatMinExp{} for experienced). It leaves \MatNGPieces{} pieces of $\G$ on dataset 1000, from \MatNGFlightsGSide{} flights and \MatNGPilotsGSide{} pilots, and \MatNAPieces{} of $\A$.

\paragraph{The MSD.} Against its controls, $\G$ has $\Hk$ of \MatSideHKG{} and $\SG$ of \MatSideHKSG{}: $\Delta(\G-\SG)$ is \MatDeltaHKG{} \MatDeltaHKGBand{} (Table~\ref{tab:matched}, Figure~\ref{fig:matched-msd}),
against the raw gap \RawGapHK{} of Section~\ref{sec:raw}; its band reaches 0. On dataset 2250, $\Hq$ gives \MatDeltaHQG{} \MatDeltaHQGBand, and $\Hloc$ at 1000~s, where the window of these longer pieces is not cut, \MatDeltaHKLongG{} \MatDeltaHKLongGBand: both bands exclude 0.
Over the lags $\Delta(\G-\SG)$ is negative at the short ones and positive at the long ones, as the raw gap: the fitted $H$ over 10 to 1000~s is lower in $\G$ than in its controls (\MatDeltaFitKG{} \MatDeltaFitKGBand),
the fitted $H$ over 10 to 2250~s higher (\MatDeltaFitTG{} \MatDeltaFitTGBand).
The band of $\Delta(\G-\SG)$ includes 0 for \MatZeroG{} of the \MatNQuant{} quantities of Figure~\ref{fig:matched-forest}.

\paragraph{Selection.} $\A$ against $\SAA$ flies the same route alone: same pilots, gliders and days as $\G$, no group. $\Delta(\A-\SAA)$ measures selection. Its $\Hk$ is \MatDeltaHKA{} \MatDeltaHKABand{} and the band includes 0 for \MatZeroA{} of the \MatNQuant{} quantities;
the difference $(\G-\SG)-(\A-\SAA)$ is \MatDeltaHKD{} \MatDeltaHKDBand{} at $\Hk$ and includes 0 for \MatZeroD{} of them.

\paragraph{The phases.} Time in climb is \MatSideClimbG\% in $\G$ and \MatSideClimbSG\% in its controls ($\Delta$ is \MatDeltaClimbG{} \MatDeltaClimbGBand), climbs per hour \MatSideCphG{} against \MatSideCphSG{} (\MatDeltaCphG{} \MatDeltaCphGBand), glide speed
\MatSideGlideG{} against \MatSideGlideSG~km/h (\MatDeltaGlideG{} \MatDeltaGlideGBand) and height lost per km of glide \MatSideSinkG{} against \MatSideSinkSG~m/km (\MatDeltaSinkG{} \MatDeltaSinkGBand): on the same route group time climbs more often and glides slower and steeper.
The bands of the time in search (\MatDeltaSearchG{} \MatDeltaSearchGBand), of the searches ending in a climb (\MatDeltaFoundG{} \MatDeltaFoundGBand) and of the climb rate (\MatDeltaRateG{} \MatDeltaRateGBand~m/s) include 0. The survival functions of the three phases
(Figure~\ref{fig:matched-survival}) lie close to each other; their bands widen in the tails, where few runs remain.

\paragraph{Robustness.} Over the \MatRobLines{} lines of Figure~\ref{fig:matched-robust}, $\Delta\Hk$ stays between \MatRobLowHK{} and \MatRobHighHK{}, with a band that includes 0 in \MatRobZeroHK{} of them, and $\Delta\Hq$, on the \MatRobLinesHQ{} lines whose long series reaches 1840~s, between
\MatRobLowHQ{} and \MatRobHighHQ, with a band that includes 0 in \MatRobZeroHQ.

\paragraph{What we keep.} On the same route, season, hour and wing group time still progresses straighter than solo flight at the long lags: $\Hq$ \MatDeltaHQG{} \MatDeltaHQGBand, $\Hloc$ at 1000~s on dataset 2250 \MatDeltaHKLongG{} \MatDeltaHKLongGBand, while on the shorter pieces of dataset 1000 $\Hk$ gives \MatDeltaHKG{} \MatDeltaHKGBand, against \RawGapHK{} raw.
It is less straight at the short lags, climbs more often and glides slower and steeper than its controls. The selection term $\Delta(\A-\SAA)$ is \MatDeltaHKA{} \MatDeltaHKABand{} at $\Hk$ and its bands include 0 for \MatZeroA{} of the \MatNQuant{} quantities:
the pilots who fly in groups, flying alone on the same routes, do not differ from their controls by more than the bands allow.
