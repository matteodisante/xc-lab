\section{Point 2: the same route, season, hour and wing}\label{sec:matched}

Does group time still differ from solo flight when the solo side flies the same route, in the same season and hour, with the same wing?

\paragraph{Controls.} For each piece of $\G$ (a stretch in a group, at least \RawShortestOne~s) the controls are the solo segments of the pool: the \MatPool{} open flights
that never had a contact of any length, on another date than the piece. A segment matches when at least \MatCover{} of the points of the piece, one every 30~s, have the pool track within
\MatRho~m, the pool flight flew them in the same direction, and the segment lasts at least as long as the dataset needs (Appendix~\ref{app:route}). The match table holds \MatMatches{} matches; \MatWithMatchG{} pieces of $\G$ and \MatWithMatchA{} pieces of $\A$ have at least one.
A caliper then keeps the matches of the same season (month), the same hour and the same wing.

\paragraph{The caliper.} The calipers are tried in the order of Table~\ref{tab:calipers}, and the chosen one is the first with at least \MatMin{} pieces of $\G$ per level on dataset 2250: month
$\pm\MatMonth$, hour $\pm\MatHour$, the same wing class (\MatMinBeg{} pieces for beginners, \MatMinExp{} for experienced). It leaves \MatNGPieces{} pieces of $\G$ on dataset 1000, from \MatNGFlightsGSide{} flights and \MatNGPilotsGSide{} pilots, and \MatNAPieces{} of $\A$.

\paragraph{The MSD.} Against its controls, $\G$ has $\Hk$ of \MatSideHKG{} and $\SG$ of \MatSideHKSG{}: $\Delta(\G-\SG)$ is \MatDeltaHKG{} \MatDeltaHKGBand{} (Table~\ref{tab:matched}, Figure~\ref{fig:matched-msd}),
against the raw gap \RawGapHK{} of Section~\ref{sec:raw}. On dataset 2250, $\Hq$ gives \MatDeltaHQG{} \MatDeltaHQGBand. The two bands include 0. The fitted $H$ is lower in $\G$ than in its controls
(\MatDeltaFitKG{} \MatDeltaFitKGBand): its $\Hloc$ is lower than that of its controls at short lags and higher at the longest lags of dataset 1000.
The band of $\Delta(\G-\SG)$ includes 0 for \MatZeroG{} of the \MatNQuant{} quantities of Figure~\ref{fig:matched-forest}.

\paragraph{Selection.} $\A$ against $\SAA$ flies the same route alone: same pilots, gliders and days as $\G$, no group. $\Delta(\A-\SAA)$ measures selection. Its $\Hk$ is \MatDeltaHKA{} \MatDeltaHKABand{} and the band includes 0 for \MatZeroA{} of the \MatNQuant{} quantities;
the difference $(\G-\SG)-(\A-\SAA)$ is \MatDeltaHKD{} \MatDeltaHKDBand{} at $\Hk$ and includes 0 for \MatZeroD{} of them.

\paragraph{The phases.} Time in climb is \MatSideClimbG\% in $\G$ and \MatSideClimbSG\% in its controls ($\Delta$ is \MatDeltaClimbG{} \MatDeltaClimbGBand), climbs per hour \MatSideCphG{} against \MatSideCphSG, glide speed
\MatSideGlideG{} against \MatSideGlideSG~km/h (\MatDeltaGlideG{} \MatDeltaGlideGBand) and the climb rate $\Delta$ is \MatDeltaRateG{} \MatDeltaRateGBand~m/s. The survival functions of the three phases
(Figure~\ref{fig:matched-survival}) overlap within their bands.

\paragraph{Robustness.} Over the \MatRobLines{} lines of Figure~\ref{fig:matched-robust}, $\Delta\Hk$ stays between \MatRobLowHK{} and \MatRobHighHK{}, with a band that includes 0 in \MatRobZeroHK{} of them, and $\Delta\Hq$ between
\MatRobLowHQ{} and \MatRobHighHQ, with a band that includes 0 in \MatRobZeroHQ.

\paragraph{What we keep.} On the same route, season, hour and wing the gap of $\Hk$ falls from \RawGapHK{} (raw) to \MatDeltaHKG{} \MatDeltaHKGBand{}, and the bands of $\Hk$, $\Hq$ and of the seven statistics include 0 for \MatZeroG{} of the \MatNQuant{}.
The selection term $\Delta(\A-\SAA)$ is \MatDeltaHKA{} at $\Hk$, with a band that includes 0. The fitted $H$ is lower in $\G$ than in its controls. The data do not exclude a gap of 0 for the
straighter progress of group time once route, season, hour and wing are fixed, nor a gap as large as the upper end of the band.
