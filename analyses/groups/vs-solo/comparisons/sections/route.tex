\section{The same route flown alone}\label{sec:route}

Groups may form on a ridge or a convergence line where any pilot progresses straight, and the
straightness would then belong to the route and not to the group. Does the gap remain when group
time and its control fly the same route?

The control is the same route flown alone, on another day. It differs from the route test of the
general report (\texttt{vs-solo/\allowbreak general}) on three points: the control may come from any
site and date other than that of the piece, and every piece of group time that has a control is used,
not a sample; the control must also have had nobody within 10~km at the same time, since a flight with
no contact in the grouping can still have flown near others; and a piece is compared with controls of
the same wing level, with the results given for experienced and beginner pilots apart.
