Group time is the straightest on every dataset (Figure~\ref{fig:open}): $\Hloc(1000~\mathrm{s})$ is
\OpenHThousandG{} in $\G$, against \OpenHThousandS{} in $\Sl$ and \OpenHThousandA{} in $\A$; at \OpenTauFixed~s,
\OpenFixedHG{} \OpenFixedHGBand{} against \OpenFixedHS{} and \OpenFixedHA. The same flights alone follow solo
flights at every lag. The bend at long lags is much weaker than on the declared open flights: at
\OpenTauLongest~s $\Hloc$ is \OpenLongestHS{} in $\Sl$ and \OpenLongestHA{} in $\A$, against \DeclLongestHS{} and
\DeclLongestHA{} before the threshold. That of $\G$ is \OpenLongestHG{} \OpenLongestHGBand, on
\OpenLongestFlightsG{} flights only.

\paragraph{What we keep.} On open flights, raw, group time progresses straighter than solo flights at every
lag, while the same flights alone move like solo flights; the threshold weakens the bend at lags of hours.
