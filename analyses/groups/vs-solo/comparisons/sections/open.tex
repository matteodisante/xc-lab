\section{Open flights}\label{sec:open}

Which flights do we compare, and why a criterion on their shape?

\paragraph{Declared open.} We start from the flights the pilot declared open on the FFVL catalogue: free
distance and distance with 1, 2 or 3 turn points; triangles, quadrilaterals and out-and-returns are
closed. The declared type agrees with the track (takeoff and landing more or less than \OpenAgreeKm~km
apart) for \OpenAgree\% of the \OpenAll{} flights. \FoldDeclared{} of them are declared open.

\paragraph{They fold.} A declared open flight is not a straight line. Its \emph{opening}, the distance from
takeoff to landing over the path flown, has a median of \FoldMedian, and of \FoldMedianLong{} for the
\FoldDeclaredLong{} flights of \FoldLongH~h or more, the ones the longest lags rest on
(Figure~\ref{fig:folding}, left). At each turn point, and along each ridge flown back, the pilot changes
direction: over lags longer than a leg the displacement grows less than the time, and $\Hloc$ falls. On
the declared open flights it is \FoldHS{} in $\Sl$, \FoldHG{} in $\G$ and \FoldHA{} in $\A$ at \FoldTau~s
(Figure~\ref{fig:folding}, right; long series on \FoldFlightsS, \FoldFlightsG{} and \FoldFlightsA{} flights).

\paragraph{The criterion.} We study the difference between group and solo flight, not the shape of the
flights. The MSD and $H$ measure displacement, so if group and solo flights fold differently they differ
through their shape, whatever the group does; the median opening is \FoldMedianGroup{} for the declared
open flights with group time and \FoldMedianSolo{} for the others. Without a criterion on the shape the
two effects are confounded. We keep the declared open flights with an opening of at least
\FoldMinOpening: \FoldStraight{} flights, \FoldShare\% of the declared open ones and \FoldShareLong\% of
those of \FoldLongH~h or more. The criterion is needed for the MSD and $H$; the statistics of the phases,
such as the glide ratio or the share of searches that end in a climb, do not depend on the shape of the
whole flight. We keep one population for every statistic, so that all the comparisons rest on the same
flights. From here on, \emph{open} means declared open with an opening of at least \FoldMinOpening.
