\subsection{The control and the measure}\label{sec:matching}

Which flights are the same route flown alone, and how is the gap with group time measured?

\paragraph{Matching.} The pieces of group time are those of Section~\ref{sec:populations}. A segment
$k$ of a solo flight is a control of the piece $g$ when all hold.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item \textbf{Route.} At least 80\% of the points of $g$, one every 30~s, lie within 500~m of the
        track of the flight of $k$, which flew them in the same direction; $k$ is the part of the track
        between its points nearest to the first and to the last of them, and lasts at least 1250~s.
  \item \textbf{Date.} Other than that of $g$, at any site.
  \item \textbf{Season and hour.} The middles of $g$ and $k$ are in months within one (circular) and
        in local hours within two.
  \item \textbf{Wing level.} The same: beginners (A, B) or experienced (C, D, CCC) in the FFVL
        catalogue.
  \item \textbf{Solo.} The flight never had a contact of any length, which is stricter than $\Sl$:
        a contact shorter than $\tau^*$ already excludes it.
  \item \textbf{Isolation.} At every point of $k$, one every 30~s, no other flight of the archive,
        alone or in a group, paraglider or hang glider, was within 10~km within one minute (the index
        of the archive holds one position per minute).
\end{enumerate}
All the matches are kept. \IsoPiecesBefore{} pieces have a match under the first five conditions
and \IsoPieces{} after the isolation; they have \IsoMatches{} matches, a median of \IsoMedianMatches{}
per piece, from \IsoSolo{} solo flights of \IsoPilotsControl{} pilots, against \IsoPilotsGroup{} pilots
on the group side.

\paragraph{Comparison.} In analogy with the zones of the general report, the stratum is the piece.
The control of a piece is the mean of the $\MSD$ of its matches, and each piece weighs the same on
both sides. With $n$ pieces and $m_g$ the matches of the piece $g$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reweight}
  \MSD_{\G}(\tau)=\frac1n\sum_{g}\MSD_g(\tau),\qquad
  \MSD_{\mathrm{ctrl}}(\tau)=\frac1n\sum_{g}\frac1{m_g}\sum_{k\in g}\MSD_k(\tau),
\end{equation}
and both sides are means of many MSDs before their logarithm. The gap is
$\Delta\Hloc(\tau)=\Hloc^{\G}(\tau)-\Hloc^{\mathrm{ctrl}}(\tau)$, positive when group time progresses
straighter. The baseline compares the same pieces with the solo flights of their site-day and hour, so
that the two gaps refer to the same group time; the share of the baseline gap that the route explains is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:explained}
  1-\frac{\Delta\Hloc^{\mathrm{route}}(1000~\mathrm{s})}{\Delta\Hloc^{\mathrm{base}}(1000~\mathrm{s})}.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Uncertainty.} Bands are 5--95\% of 1000 bootstrap draws that resample the pilots and
the dependency blocks of the grouping, independently, and recompute every statistic; each difference has
its own band. The thin lines of the figures are the range when one year at a time is left out. The
baseline and the explained share are point estimates.

\paragraph{What we keep.} The control is the same route, on another day, in the same season, hour and
wing level, flown by a solo pilot with nobody within 10~km. One gap, $\Delta\Hloc$, is read at
1000~s and compared with the baseline of the same pieces.
