\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We compare paraglider group time with solo flight on open flights, four ways: raw; against solo flights on the
same route, season, hour and wing; by how much each of those factors accounts for the raw gap; and against the
same flight before and after its group time. All bands come from a two-way bootstrap of pilots and blocks, whose 90\% coverage we check by simulation on the designs of the data.
Raw, group time progresses straighter than solo flight at the long lags: $\Hk$ is \RawHKOneG{} in $\G$ and \RawHKOneS{} in $\Sl$, a gap of \RawGapHK{} \RawGapHKBand.
On the same route, season, hour and wing the gap of $\Hk$ is \MatDeltaHKG{} \MatDeltaHKGBand{} on pieces of at least \RawShortestOne~s (against controls on the route, not against all solo flights), while on longer pieces $\Hq$ gives \MatDeltaHQG{} \MatDeltaHQGBand; group time also climbs more and glides slower and steeper than its controls; but the same pilots alone show the same glide deficits, and at $\Hq$ they show \MatDeltaHQA{} \MatDeltaHQABand, as much as the group, so on the same route the group effect cannot be told from who flies in groups. Net of this selection only the time in climb stays clear of 0 (\MatDeltaClimbD{} \MatDeltaClimbDBand{} points).
Taking the factors apart, the route accounts for most of the difference between group time and controls drawn from any route (time in climb, glide speed, height lost per km, and $\Hq$). Around the group, the
drift-free effect $g$ of $\Hk$ is \PreGHK{} \PreGHKBand, close to the same-route gap at 1000~s on the longer pieces (\MatDeltaHKLongG), and the same for windows from \PreWinBLen{} to \PreWinDLen~s. This design keeps the same flight, so it does not depend on who flies. Time in search, climb rate, glide speed and height lost per km of glide have opposite signs in the matched and in the before-and-after comparison; net of selection, time in search, climb rate and glide speed agree in sign with $g$ (bands that include 0).
\end{abstract}
