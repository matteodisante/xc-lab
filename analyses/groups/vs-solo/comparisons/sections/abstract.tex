\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We compare paraglider group time with solo flight on open flights, four ways: raw; against solo flights on the
same route, season, hour and wing; by how much each of those factors accounts for the raw gap; and against the
same flight before and after its group time. Raw, group time progresses straighter than solo flight:
$\Hk$ is \RawHKOneG{} in $\G$ and \RawHKOneS{} in $\Sl$. On the same route, season, hour and wing the gap is
\MatDeltaHKG{} \MatDeltaHKGBand, a band that includes 0; so do the bands of \MatZeroG{} of the \MatNQuant{} quantities. Taking the factors apart,
the route carries the largest part of the difference of time in climb, glide speed and height lost per km of glide. Around the group, the
drift-free effect $g$ of $\Hk$ is \PreGHK{} \PreGHKBand. Time in search, climb rate, glide speed and height lost per km of glide have opposite signs in the matched and in the before-and-after comparison.
\end{abstract}
