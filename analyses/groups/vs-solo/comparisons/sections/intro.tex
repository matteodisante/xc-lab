\section{Aim}\label{sec:intro}

Does a paraglider move differently in a group than alone? This report answers with a series of
comparisons of group time with solo flight. Each has its own control, and a result is read together
with the number of flights or pieces behind it, given in every table and figure.

All the populations are paragliders on open flights. Section~\ref{sec:populations} defines them, counts them and maps
them, Section~\ref{sec:raw} compares them as they are, over lags up to hours and by the size of the
group, Section~\ref{sec:zones} repeats the comparison in the zones with the most group flights, and
Section~\ref{sec:route} compares group time with the same route flown alone.
