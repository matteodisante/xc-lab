\section{Aim}\label{sec:intro}

Does a paraglider move differently in a group than alone? This report answers with a series of
comparisons of group time with solo flight. Each has its own control, and a result is read together
with the number of flights or pieces behind it, given in every table and figure.

All the populations are paragliders. Section~\ref{sec:populations} defines them, Section~\ref{sec:open}
shows that the flights declared open fold back on themselves and keeps the straight ones, counts them and
maps them, Section~\ref{sec:raw} compares the populations as they are, over lags up to hours and by the
size of the group, Section~\ref{sec:zones} repeats the comparison in the zones with the most group
flights, and Section~\ref{sec:route} compares group time with the same route flown alone.
