Of the 154\,598 paraglider flights with a logger of at most 10~s, 33\,501 (21.7\%) fly in a group at some point; of the 6\,039 hang-glider flights, 418 (6.9\%). Group time is 12.8\% of all paraglider flight time after the departure, and on average 45\% of the time of a group flight. The 36\,575 pair intervals last 40 to 96~min (10th to 90th percentile; median 52~min) and form 40\,051 groups in 17\,169 episodes. Half of the episodes have two flights, 1\% have more than 9, the largest has 53. By the size of the group, the paraglider group time is: 2 pilots 64\%, 3 pilots 19\%, 4 pilots 8\%, 5-6 pilots 6\%, 7+ pilots 3\% (the largest group has 27 pilots). In contact, one pilot is behind the other by a median of 25~s (75\% within 45~s).
