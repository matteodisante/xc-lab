## Goal

A full check of `story.tex` (compiled `story.pdf`, about 42 pages): **correctness** of every statement and
number, and **form**: one fixed, clear logical structure, so that the document reads as a story told in
points. The current version is liked (schematic, readable): improve it, do not rewrite its voice.

Spend tokens carefully: Sonnet agents for mechanical checks, the main session (Opus) for decisions and edits;
no rereading of the whole document at every step; agents report lists of issues, they do not edit.

## The fixed structure of a step

Explained once at the start of the document, then used everywhere:

**Q** (the question) → **Why** (why we ask it, what it would change) → **Tool** (the analysis, and why this
tool) → **Result** (figures, tables, numbers) → **Observation** (what we see) → **Deduction** (what follows) →
**Take home**.

- *Hypothesis* and *Comment* only where they carry something; method boxes (`method`, `problembox`) stay apart
  from the observations.
- Today the document also uses How, Problem, Rule, Test, Where, Left out: map each to the fixed sequence (e.g.
  Test → Tool, Problem → a problem box or Why) and drop the labels nobody needs.
- Each chapter's question must follow from the previous chapter's take home; say so in its Why.
- If a better logical order of chapters or steps exists, propose it before moving anything.

## The three passes

1. **Structure** (main session): apply the fixed step sequence; list the label changes and any reordering;
   check that each Why links to the previous take home.
2. **Correctness** (Sonnet agents in parallel, one group of chapters each: 0–2, 3–5, 6–7, 8–10 + appendices):
   every number against its macro and source JSON/table; every claim against the figure or data it cites
   (signs, "within the bands", "larger in", "about"); macros that are cited but not defined; numbers written by
   hand that should be macros. Output: a list `chapter · line · issue · evidence · proposed fix`.
3. **Clarity** (one adversarial reader, Sonnet, with NO project context: give it only the PDF text): where it does
   not understand, where a deduction does not follow from the results, where a question is not motivated, where
   terms are used before being defined. Output: the same list format.

Then the main session decides, edits in small steps, recompiles, and reports to the user what changed.
