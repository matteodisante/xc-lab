\section{Conclusion on universality}\label{sec:conclusion}

Is $H$ the same for paragliders and hang gliders?

\paragraph{What the data say.} On open flights, at lags from $10$ s to $1000$ s, the paragliders have the larger $H$ than the
hang gliders. The difference $\Hpara-\Hhang$ is \UniDHAllAfixed{} for all terrain, between \UniDHPlainsAfixed{} (plains) and
\UniDHLowAfixed{} (low mountains) by class, \UniRegDHAlpsAfixed{} in the Alps, and it keeps this sign in all the \UniForestN{}
combinations of population and design (Fig.~\ref{fig:forest}); the band excludes 0 in \UniForestPos{} of them. It does not come from
the mix of terrains, nor from the choice between a fixed population and all flights. It is not resolved at the longest lag
of the raw comparison (\UniDHAllAlongest{} \UniDHBandAllAlongest), where the hang glider band is wide.

\paragraph{How large it is.} The scale to compare with is the effect of the ground on $H$ within a discipline: from plains to high mountains
$H$ goes from \UniHFirstPlainsAfixed{} to \UniHFirstHighAfixed{} for paragliders, from \UniHSecondPlainsAfixed{} to
\UniHSecondHighAfixed{} for hang gliders, and for paragliders the difference of $H$ between the Alps and Channel Coast is \UniBetweenParaDHAlpsChannelAfixed.
The difference between the gliders, \UniForestMin{} to \UniForestMax, is smaller than these but of the same order. Both disciplines
follow the same ordering with the ground and the same shape of $\alpha(\tau)$ (Figs.~\ref{fig:strat-A}, \ref{fig:regions-ph}).

\paragraph{Answer.} $H$ is universal to a few hundredths, and not to the precision of the bands: the two gliders share the dependence
on the ground, and $\Hpara-\Hhang$ lies between \UniForestMin{} and \UniForestMax. Why they sit lower is not tested
here (speed of the glider, the way thermals are used, the sites and the pilots are all possible). Two
limits: the hang glider populations are small (\UniFunnelHangOpen{} flights, \UniFunnelHangPilots{} pilots, and fewer in
each class), and the comparison is made on declared open flights only.

\keep{$\Hpara-\Hhang$ is positive, \UniForestMin{} to \UniForestMax, in every population and design; it is smaller than the effect of the ground
on $H$ and of the same order. $H$ is universal to a few hundredths.}
